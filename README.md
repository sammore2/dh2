## Open Content and SRD 2.0 Rules
* Action dice mechanics (Duality Dice: Hope and Fear d12s).
* Traits: Agility, Strength, Finesse, Instinct, Presence, Knowledge.
* Damage Thresholds (Minor, Major, Severe), Hit Points (HP) and Stress.
* Domain Cards, abilities and equipment.
* Adversary structure and action difficulties.
